## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on **Exploratory Data Analysis of Telco Customer Churn data** using Python.

The analysis explores customer demographics, tenure, contract types, internet services, additional services, payment methods, and churn behavior. Different statistical and visualization techniques are used to identify patterns and relationships within the dataset.

The project demonstrates a practical **data analytics workflow — from data understanding and cleaning to visualization and business-oriented analysis.**

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Understand the structure and characteristics of the dataset.
* Perform data cleaning and quality checks.
* Analyze customer demographic characteristics.
* Explore customer tenure and contract types.
* Analyze internet and additional services.
* Examine payment methods and customer behavior.
* Analyze the distribution of customer churn.
* Compare categorical variables using percentage-based analysis.
* Create clear and informative visualizations.
* Extract meaningful observations from customer data.

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Customer Churn Analysis

Analyzed the overall distribution of customers based on the `Churn` variable.

### 2. Demographic Analysis

Explored customer characteristics such as:

* Gender
* Senior Citizen
* Partner
* Dependents

### 3. Tenure Analysis

Analyzed customer tenure to understand how customer duration relates to churn behavior.

### 4. Contract Analysis

Examined different contract categories:

* Month-to-month
* One year
* Two year

### 5. Internet Service Analysis

Analyzed customer usage across different internet service categories.

### 6. Additional Services Analysis

The project analyzes:

* Phone Service
* Multiple Lines
* Internet Service
* Online Security
* Online Backup
* Device Protection
* Tech Support
* Streaming TV
* Streaming Movies

### 7. Payment Method Analysis

Customer payment methods were explored to understand their distribution and relationship with churn.

### 8. Percentage-Based Analysis

Percentage-based and **100% stacked bar charts** were used to compare churn levels within categories.

This approach makes comparisons easier when different categories contain different numbers of customers.
